1. Scope

The project and its tools are used only by their owner. It is not a public service and has no other users.

2. Published content

The app only posts content that the owner has created and reviewed, to the owner’s own accounts, after the owner has authorized it.

3. Not investment advice

All content is educational. Results shown in videos are simulations based on historical data and do not guarantee future results. Nothing is a recommendation to buy or sell.

4. Revoking access

The owner may revoke the app’s access to any social account at any time from that platform’s settings.
